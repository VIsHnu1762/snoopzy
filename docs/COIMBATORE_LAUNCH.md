  Why Coimbatore first
  Coimbatore is a near-ideal test city for Snoopzy: a dense student population across 100+
  engineering colleges, newly revived lakefronts and big public parks, a mild climate with a
  long dry season, and a manageable size where one team can personally reach the first
  squads.

       Players: large student population concentrated in a few campus belts (Peelamedu,
       Saravanampatti, Ettimadai and the western colleges).
       Places: well-known parks (VOC Park, Race Course, Gandhi Park, Bharathi Park) and
       Smart City lakefronts (Ukkadam Periyakulam, Valankulam) with walking paths.
       Weather: dry and comfortable from late December to early March; evenings playable
       much of the year; wettest in October–November.
       Reach: a tier-2 city where word of mouth across a few campuses can make Snoopzy
       the thing to play — cheaper and faster than a metro launch.
       Watch-outs: wild-elephant movement on the western foothill fringe, heavy-rain weeks
       in Oct–Nov, and the need for campus permissions.

  How to approach a campus

   1. Find a student club (gaming, coding, NSS, fitness) to host a "Snoopzy night" as their
      event.
   2. Get written permission from the student-affairs office: date, time, area, number of
      students, safety plan (our safety card + a student marshal).
   3. Mark buildings, roads and restricted areas as no-go in the room before play.
   4. Run 3 short rounds, then show the recap on a projector — that's the moment people
      share.
   5. Leave behind a QR poster for the next week's squad challenge.

  Local safety and permissions
  Coimbatore has one risk most cities don't: wild elephants on the western foothill fringe.
  Forest officials have reported more than 50 wild elephants camped in forest areas of
  Madukkarai, Sadivayal, Thondamuthur, Maruthamalai and Thadagam in the foothills (ETV
  Bharat, Oct 2025), and herds have entered residential areas near Vadavalli (Save the
  Elephants).

  Go-to-market in Coimbatore
  Win one campus completely, then use that campus's squads to spread to the next. Paid
  ads come last.


  Phases
   1. Founder squads (weeks 1–2 of beta). Your own friend groups play 3+ times a week; fix
      everything that breaks. Record clips.
   2. First campus (weeks 3–6). One large campus (Saravanampatti cluster or Amrita) with a
       host club. Weekly "Snoopzy Night", projector recap, squad leaderboard on a poster.
   3. Three campuses (weeks 7–12). Inter-college squad challenge: best squad from each
      campus plays a public match at a scouted lakefront or park.
   4. IT corridor (from month 3). Team-outing kit for Saravanampatti and Vilankurichi IT
      companies: Capture the Flag on office campuses, Friday evening events.
   5. Families (Pongal / summer holidays). Kannamoochi family preset promoted through
      apartment communities.

       Tamil + English copy, with a few Kongu-region jokes in roast lines (tested with local

       city's popular local name).
