# Snoopzy — Brand Identity Ideas

Oct 29, 2026 · @vishnu

## Brand in one screen

**Snoopzy turns your real streets into a game board for your squad.** It feels like being a kid again — heart pounding behind a tree — with the tension of a battle royale and the jokes of your group chat.

| Element          | Snoopzy                                                                       |
|------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Essence          | *The thrill of being almost found.*                                           |
| Promise          | Get your friends outside and make a story you'll talk about all week.         |
| Audience (first) | Friend squads, 18–30, in Indian college and IT cities                         |
| Personality      | Sneaky, cheeky, warm, a little dramatic, never mean                           |
| Feeling we want  | Breathless laughter after a near-miss                                         |
| Tagline          | **Hide & Seek IRL**                                                           |
| Brand idea       | **Peek.** Every touchpoint plays with eyes, peeking, being seen and not seen. |

The recurring motif is **two eyes peeking** — from a logo, a mascot, a loading screen, a notification. It is simple, works at every size, and is instantly about hiding and seeking.

## Story and positioning

**The story:** Every Indian grew up playing hide and seek in a street, a colony or a school ground — Kannamoochi, Chhupam Chhupai, Lukochuri. Then phones arrived, and play moved indoors onto screens. Snoopzy uses the phone to bring that play back outside: the phone becomes the referee, and the city becomes the map again.

**Positioning statement:** For friend groups who want something more exciting than another night scrolling, Snoopzy is the real-world hide & seek and battle royale game that turns any park or campus into a live arena — unlike online games, you play with real friends, in real places, with your real legs.

| Compared with                          | They are                                         | Snoopzy is                                        |
|----------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Online battle royales                  | Screen-only, strangers, virtual maps             | Outdoors, your squad, your streets                |
| Location games (collecting, exploring) | Solo progress, collecting things                 | Friends competing with each other, face to face   |
| Location-sharing apps                  | Always-on tracking for safety or family          | Location only during a match, only for the game   |
| Plain hide and seek                    | No referee, arguments over tags, forgotten after | Fair tags, timers, radar, recap and squad history |

**Three words to own:** *IRL. Squad. Peek.*

## Personality and voice

Snoopzy talks like the funniest friend in the group: quick, teasing, dramatic about small things, and on your side.

| Trait                 | Sounds like                                 | Not like                                         |
|-----------------------|---------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Sneaky                | "They didn't see you. Yet."                 | Creepy or stalker-ish ("We're watching you")     |
| Cheeky                | "Ran 2 km. Caught nobody. Iconic."          | Insults about looks, body, caste, gender, region |
| Dramatic              | "The zone is closing. This is not a drill." | Real violence words (kill, shoot, dead)          |
| Warm                  | "Squad's waiting. You in?"                  | Guilt-tripping ("Your friends are disappointed") |
| Clear when it matters | "Hold for 2 s to send SOS."                 | Jokes in safety, permission or privacy screens   |

**Rule:** fun everywhere, except safety, privacy and money screens — those are plain and calm.

Short, playful, and built around peeking and snooping. Avoid weapon or violence words; this is a tag game, not a shooter.

| Category     | Names                                                                                                         |

## Logo directions

**Recommendation: A + B together.** The peeking wordmark (the "oo" in Snoopzy as two eyes) is the main logo; the radar eye is the app icon and small-size mark. Both say "seeking" at a glance.

| Direction             | Idea                                                                    | Strengths                                                                                            | Risks                                                                         |
|-----------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| A. Peeking wordmark   | The two o's become eyes glancing sideways; pupils can move in animation | Uses the name itself; instantly memorable; animates beautifully (eyes dart, blink, look at the user) | Must stay readable as "Snoopzy" at small sizes                                |
| B. Radar eye          | An eye whose iris is a radar sweep                                      | Perfect app icon; ties to the in-game radar                                                          | Can feel techy or surveillance-like if too cold — keep it rounded and playful |
| C. Peeking pin        | A map pin peeking over a wall with two eyes                             | Says "location game" clearly                                                                         | Map-pin logos are common; less distinctive                                    |
| D. Shrinking zone "S" | An S drawn from concentric shrinking rings                              | Battle royale reference                                                                              | Less about hiding; harder to read                                             |

**Logo rules (to brief a designer):** works in one colour; reads at 24 px; eyes always look sideways (never straight at the viewer, which reads as watching); rounded, friendly geometry.

## Mascot ideas

A mascot gives the brand a face for stickers, loading screens and reactions. It must be **original** and must not look like any existing game or cartoon character.

| Idea                  | Description                                                                                                     | Why it fits                                                                                        | Watch-outs                                                                                                 |
|-----------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Zee** (recommended) | A small hooded shadow with only two big peeking eyes visible under the hood; body changes colour with your team | Pure "peek" idea; no face to localise; easy to animate (eyes dart, hood pulls tighter when hiding) | Keep it cute, not spooky; avoid a bean-shaped body, which is strongly associated with another popular game |
| Pinny                 | A map pin with legs that hides behind things                                                                    | Clearly a location game                                                                            | Map-pin mascots are common                                                                                 |
| The Dabba             | A tin can with eyes, from the Dabba Spy street game                                                             | Strong Indian nostalgia hook                                                                       | Less recognisable outside India                                                                            |

**Avoid dog or beagle mascots.** The name Snoopzy already sounds close to a world-famous cartoon dog; a dog mascot would invite confusion and legal trouble.

**Zee's expressions (sticker pack v1):** peeking, *shh*, caught (eyes wide), smug (survived), sweating (zone closing), heart eyes (revived), sleepy (lobby waiting), detective (seeker).

## Colour palette

**Night-mode first.** The game is played outdoors, often at golden hour and dusk, and the HUD must read in a glance. A deep ink background with one electric accent beats a rainbow. Each colour has one job.

| Name        | Hex      | Role                                                          | Rule                                          |
|-------------|----------|---------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Night Ink   | \#0E0F1A | Main background, map base                                     | Default for app and posters                   |
| Shadow      | \#1B1D2E | Cards, sheets, panels                                         | One step above Night Ink                      |
| Radar Lime  | \#C6FF3D | **Primary accent**: radar, Tag button, logo eyes, key actions | Only one lime element per screen should shout |
| Ping Pink   | \#FF3D8B | Seekers / hunters, pings, "you're cooked"                     | Means danger is near                          |
| Signal Blue | \#3DA9FF | Hiders / your team, teammates' dots                           | Means safety and allies                       |
| Zone Violet | \#8B5CF6 | Shrinking zone ring, Battle Royale                            | Only for the zone                             |
| Alert Amber | \#FFB020 | Warnings: weak GPS, low battery, out of bounds soon           | Never decorative                              |
| SOS Red     | \#FF4D4D | SOS and true errors only                                      | Reserved; never for fun                       |
| Paper       | \#F5F3EE | Light mode, printed posters, merch                            | Warm off-white, not pure white                |

**Accessibility:** pink vs blue are the two team colours because they stay distinct for the most common colour-blindness types better than red vs green; every team colour is also paired with a shape (circle vs diamond). Check every text/background pair against WCAG contrast before final sign-off.

## Typography

Chunky and rounded for the game voice; clean and highly readable for everything a running player must read. All suggestions are open-licence fonts available on Google Fonts (check licences before final use).

| Use                                          | Suggested font                         | Why                                            |
|----------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Display (logo, big moments: "YOU'RE COOKED") | **Unbounded** (bold, wide)             | Playful, game-like, distinctive at large sizes |
| UI and body                                  | **Manrope** or **Inter**               | Very readable at small sizes and in sunlight   |
| Numbers (timers, distances)                  | Same UI font with tabular figures      | Digits don't jump as the timer counts          |
| Tamil                                        | **Noto Sans Tamil** or **Mukta Malar** | Matches the clean UI font weight               |
| Hindi / Devanagari                           | **Noto Sans Devanagari** or **Mukta**  | Same                                           |
| Malayalam                                    | **Noto Sans Malayalam**                | Same                                           |

**Type rules:** big moments in display caps, 2–4 words max; HUD numbers at least 28 pt; never use display font for body text; every screen tested in Tamil and English because Tamil words are often longer.

## Icons, illustration, map style and motion

### Icons

- Rounded line icons, 2 px stroke at 24 px, rounded ends. Filled versions only for the active state.

- Custom icons for game objects: Revive Shield, Armor, Ghost, Decoy, radar, dabba, safe house, no-go.

### Illustration

- **Sticker style:** flat colour, thick white outline (like a sticker), slight drop shadow on Paper backgrounds.

- Scenes show **real Indian places** — a banyan tree, a college corridor, a lake promenade, an apartment courtyard — not generic Western suburbs.

- People are diverse in skin tone, body type, clothing (kurtas, hoodies, saris, jerseys) and ability.

### Map style

- Custom dark map: Night Ink land, muted Shadow roads, soft green for parks, deep blue for water.

- No shop or business labels during matches (less clutter, fewer distractions).

- Play area glows faintly in Radar Lime; no-go zones are hatched in Alert Amber; the zone ring is Zone Violet.

### Motion

| Moment      | Motion                                                                    |
|-------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| App open    | Logo eyes look left, right, then at the play button                       |
| Radar pulse | A ring expands and fades every pulse; blips glow then fade                |
| Tag         | Screen shakes 150 ms, colour flashes Ping Pink, "YOU'RE COOKED" stamps in |
| Revive      | Ring fills over 5 s, then a burst of Signal Blue                          |
| Zone shrink | Violet ring tightens smoothly with a 3-2-1 count                          |
| Recap       | Awards drop in one by one like stickers being slapped on                  |

Keep animations short (under 400 ms for UI) and offer a *reduce motion* setting.

## Sound and haptics identity

Players often have the phone in a pocket, so **sound and vibration carry the brand as much as visuals**.

| Element        | Idea                                                                                                        |
|----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Sonic logo     | Two quick notes — a rising "peek" and a playful "boo" pop — under 1 second; plays on app open and in videos |
| Seeker release | A short rising siren, more playful than alarming                                                            |
| Radar ping     | Soft sonar "blip", quieter for hiders (so they aren't given away)                                           |
| Tag            | Comic "bonk" + crowd "ohhh"; the *"You're cooked"* voice line (optional)                                    |
| Revive         | Ice-cracking then a bright chime ("Ice-Water" reference)                                                    |
| Zone warning   | Low pulsing hum that rises as the countdown ends                                                            |
| Win            | Short brass-and-drums sting; different stings for hiders and seekers                                        |

### Haptic patterns

| Pattern                 | Meaning                    |
|-------------------------|----------------------------|
| Heartbeat (slow → fast) | A hunter is getting closer |
| One long buzz           | Out of bounds / no-go      |
| Three short taps        | Zone shrinking soon        |
| Double tap              | Revive started on you      |
| Strong single thump     | You've been tagged         |

**Rules:** every sound has a matching vibration (for silent mode); hiders' phones get a *stealth audio* default (sounds only in earbuds); voice lines available in English and Tamil first.

## Guardrails and next steps

### Guardrails

- **Not a shooter.** No guns, crosshairs, blood or "kill" language anywhere — even in Battle Royale. We tag, lock, freeze and snoop.

- **Not surveillance.** Eyes look sideways and playful, never stare at the viewer; no "we see you" copy; location messaging always says *only during the match*.

- **Safety looks serious.** SOS, permissions and privacy screens use plain language and calm colours.

- **Inclusive humour.** Roasts are about game moments only.

- **Original and clear of others' IP.** No borrowing from existing games' characters, logos or art; avoid any dog mascot given the name's closeness to a famous cartoon dog; search trademarks before finalising the logo.

- **Local, not stereotyped.** Regional flavour comes from real local players, reviewed before release.
