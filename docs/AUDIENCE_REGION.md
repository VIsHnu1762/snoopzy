  Summary
  Snoopzy's core loop (hide, chase, tag, rescue) is one of the most universal games humans
  play, so it can fit almost every age and region. What changes is the rules preset, the
  safety settings, and the law. Launch with adults 18–30 in South Indian college cities, then
  widen.

       Best first audience: college and early-career friend groups, 18–30. They have dense
       friend networks, campuses and parks nearby, Android phones, and evenings free.
       Next: office teams (team outings), families and cousins (festival gatherings, with an
       adult host), and fitness groups.
       Later, with extra work: teens 13–17 (parental consent, guardian view), kids under 13 (a
       separate, very restricted mode), schools and events.
       Region fit in India: strongest in cities with big student populations, parks and a long
       dry season. South India is a good first base; North India has a strong winter play
       window.
       Global: the format travels well, but age laws (US COPPA, EU GDPR age of consent,
       India DPDP) and climate decide when each region is worth entering.


  Age groups
  Every age can enjoy the game, but the law, the safety risk and the physical demand
  change with age. In India everyone under 18 counts as a child under the DPDP Act, so v1 is
  18+ only.

  Launch order
  One audience and one city at a time, each stage unlocked by a clear signal that the
  previous one works.

   1. Coimbatore college squads (18–24), Android. Hide & Seek MVP, English + Tamil copy,
      Kannamoochi preset. Move on when: squads play again within 7 days without
       prompting.
   2. Coimbatore working adults and office teams (25–40). Office-campus and team-
      outing presets, Capture the Flag. Move on when: repeat corporate bookings or office
      squads.
   3. Other South Indian tier-2 and campus cities (e.g. Kochi, Madurai, Tiruchirappalli,
      Mysuru), then Bengaluru and Chennai via college fests. Move on when: organic installs
      from cities we didn't market in.

   4. Families (adult-hosted) during festivals (Pongal, Onam, Diwali, summer holidays) with
       Treasure Hunt and family presets.
   5. Teens 13–17 once verifiable parental consent and Guardian view are built and reviewed
      by a lawyer.
   6. North, West and East India with Hindi, Marathi and Bengali presets (Chhupam
       Chhupai, Lukochuri), timed to each region's cool season.
   7. iOS, then diaspora hubs (UAE, Singapore), then markets that need GDPR or COPPA
      work.

  Kids under 13 stay out of scope until there is a dedicated, very restricted family mode.
